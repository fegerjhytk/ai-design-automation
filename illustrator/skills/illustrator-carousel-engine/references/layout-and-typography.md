# Layout and typography

Use explicit dimensions, style references, and live manual refinements as the baseline. Inspect the reference's margins, text anchors, hierarchy, colors, text treatment, and spacing. Apply those patterns to the requested slides while keeping existing intentional variations.

## New portrait carousels

- The standard portrait preset is 1080 × 1350 with a 108 pt inset on each side and an 864 pt text column in Illustrator. Compute bounds from each artboard. For other sizes, use the supplied guides or derive a suitable inset. Verify positive usable width and height before creating guides.
- In a new standard document, keep editable slide artwork in one content layer, with clear per-slide grouping where it helps editing. Put native safe-area guides on a separate locked layer. When editing an existing document, use its established layer structure and reuse matching guides.
- Align related text, rules, and accents to the same column anchor. Use physical centering for an intentionally centered layout. Keep required marks, logos, and QR artwork at their approved scale and proportions.

## Lisa Kristine Authority Carousel

This project-specific design system comes from Lisa Kristine’s Authority Carousel series. Keep the series typefaces, their roles, and the established way images are presented consistent across Lisa Kristine carousels. Layout, text line breaks, and element placement can change with the content and the slide’s role. For other clients or series, use their own briefs and references.

- The tested Carousel 2 cover uses a full-bleed speaker portrait, a lower-left Palatino Bold lead-in, a cream Fraunces 72pt SemiBold headline, and an amber Fraunces 72pt SemiBold Italic accent. Keep longer lines over readable areas of the portrait.
- Use the relevant series artboard to verify the shared image treatment and the intended layout for each cover or inner slide. Match the requested slide’s framing and placement without assuming that every slide uses the same composition. Warm cream `#F5EFEB`, charcoal `#1A1A1A`, and amber `#E1B45F` are existing series options when a specific reference gives no colors.
- Resolve actual installed names and weights before placement. When a required font is missing, choose a fallback only if permitted by the brief and disclose the substitution.

## Editable copy and fit

1. Preserve the supplied wording, punctuation, numbers, sequence, and meaningful paragraph boundaries. Add labels, numbering, icons, or supplemental copy only when requested. Normalize only layout-induced line breaks for copy comparison and preserve authored breaks where they carry meaning.
2. Prefer point type for short single-line labels, often seven words or fewer, when they fit at the intended size. Use area type for wrapping, longer copy, or constrained columns. Test rendered width even for a single long word. Use one editable frame for a continuous block with multiple styles where practical, preserving separate frames when the document or layout needs them.
3. In editorial headlines, favor lines of roughly two to four words where the language and phrase boundaries support it. Choose line breaks by meaning and rendered balance. Treat the standard body size bands as starting points: 32–34 pt with 40–44 pt leading for short copy, 28–30 pt with 36–40 pt leading for medium copy, and 24–26 pt with 30–34 pt leading for longer copy.
4. For new text, keep horizontal and vertical glyph scale at 100%, baseline shift at 0 pt, and character rotation at 0° unless the brief calls for a deliberate effect. Check the frame transform as well as character settings. Preserve intentional superscripts, rotations, and other formatting on existing text outside the requested changes.
5. Fit by widening or deepening the type area within the safe bounds, adjusting natural line breaks, or changing font size and leading proportionally. If a readable result still does not fit, use an allowed alternate layout or slide break, or report the fit conflict. Verify the composed content and inspect the final word, text boundaries, and neighboring elements at reading size.
